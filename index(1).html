Privacy Policy

App: Mbegu Link (com.zibo.mbegulink) · Effective date: November 4, 2026

Mbegu Link (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is provided by Zibo, based in Nairobi, Kenya. This policy explains what information the app collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account and profile details you enter, such as your name, contact details and location or community information.
  • Content you add to the app, such as registry entries, community posts or details, and any photos you choose to upload.
  • Messages or information you send us when you contact us for support.

Information collected automatically

  • Crash reports and diagnostics: when the app crashes or hits an error, we collect technical information such as crash logs, device model, operating system version, app version, and a random installation identifier. This is collected through Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Basic request information (such as IP address and timestamps) is processed by our servers when the app communicates with them.

Device permissions

  • Photos and media (or camera): only when you choose to attach or take a picture inside the app. The app does not access your photos in the background.
  • Internet access: needed to load and save data.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the app and its features.
  • To create and manage your profile and the content you share.
  • To find and fix crashes and improve stability and performance.
  • To respond to your questions and support requests.
  • To keep the app and our servers secure and to prevent misuse.

3. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers that help us run the app: Google Firebase (crash reporting and diagnostics) and our cloud hosting provider Render (hosting the app’s backend and its data). These providers process data on our behalf and under their own privacy terms.
  • Other users, where you choose to share content in community or registry features that are visible to others.
  • Legal reasons, if required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.

4. Data storage and security

Data is transmitted between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including where our service providers operate.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the app. Crash and diagnostic data is retained by Firebase for a limited period under its standard settings. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the address below, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Third-party links and services

The app may link to or rely on third-party services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of those services, including Google’s at policies.google.com/privacy and Firebase’s at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app or by other reasonable means.
